The next card now appears the moment you rate or Radio moves on, instead of after a short pause. When you reach the last card in your queue, the next batch of new cards is added while you're still on it, so there's no pause after you submit (new cards arrive 3 at a time by default, adjustable in the learning settings), and having the learning view open in two tabs no longer adds that batch twice. Translations and audio are prepared 20 sentences ahead in every mode, including Radio, and the home screen re-checks upcoming sentences each time you return to it.
