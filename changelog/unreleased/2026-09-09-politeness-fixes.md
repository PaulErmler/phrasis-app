A round of fixes to the new politeness levels. Cards no longer get stuck on
"updating" after you pick a level, audio always speaks the sentence you can see
(including the audio button in a collection preview and "regenerate audio" on
Spanish (Mixed) cards), and library search matches the words your card actually
shows. A card you kept on an earlier wording now keeps it when we correct the
sentence's speaker. The speaker chip reads "male speaker" or "female speaker",
which is what it always described, and on a Spanish (Mixed) course the
politeness chip names the right form for the dialect you are looking at. Setup
no longer re-asks for a level you already picked, and writing feedback grades
your answer against the politeness level of the card instead of a neutral one.
