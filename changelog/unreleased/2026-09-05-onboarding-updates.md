Onboarding now asks which language apps you've used before (Anki, Glossika, Clozemaster, Babbel, Duolingo, something else, or none), and new courses start with three cards instead of five so the first lesson opens with a shorter queue. Two fixes along the way: coming back to an unfinished setup no longer sends you to the level picker again after you took the placement test, and unticking "other" on the apps question clears the text you typed for it.
