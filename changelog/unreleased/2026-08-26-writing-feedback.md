Writing feedback now labels a different word or phrase as Word choice (not Word order), can call out punctuation on its own, and plays the matching alternative when you submit — each speaker button plays the sentence next to it. Coach notes appear in your first base language and explain the wording you were writing — not a phrase in your source language. Also-correct notes talk about how natural your wording is. Enter still takes you to the next card after the notes appear. While the coach is checking, the notes card fills in so the wait matches the result. The edit dialog now lists the answers saved as also-correct for a sentence, and you can reword or remove them there.
