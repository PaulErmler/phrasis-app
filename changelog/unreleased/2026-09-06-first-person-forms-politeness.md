Every course can now choose which first-person forms (masculine, feminine, or both alternating) and which politeness levels its sentences use, asked during onboarding and course creation and adjustable later in the course's language settings. Japanese and Korean learners get real casual, polite (です・ます / 해요체) and formal options; other languages show their own two forms (du/Sie, tu/vous). Each card, and each sentence in a collection preview, shows the gender and politeness of the wording it displays. Existing cards keep their wording and voice.
