Every course can now choose which politeness levels its sentences use, asked during onboarding and course creation and adjustable later in the course's language settings, where a change shows at once. Japanese and Korean learners get real casual, polite (です・ます / 해요체) and formal options; other languages have a casual and a formal form (du/Sie, tu/vous). Everywhere a sentence is shown — the review card, the library, a collection preview and the sentences behind a word in your stats — it carries a masculine or feminine chip for the voice it is spoken in (its wording follows that voice in languages that mark it) and, where a level shows, a politeness chip. Existing cards keep their wording and voice.
