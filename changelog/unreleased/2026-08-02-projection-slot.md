The home screen now shows where your learning is heading: next to your daily goal, a rotating stat projects your progress — how many words you'll know by the end of the year, when you'll reach the next level, what one session is worth in new words, and more. It updates with your real pace and cycles automatically; tap it to see the next one. It unlocks after your first 10 minutes of study, so the numbers are based on a real pace rather than a single card.
