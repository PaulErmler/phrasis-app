A new 7-day forecast on the home screen shows how much review time each of
the coming days will bring — including the cards that come back after you
review them — and lets you preview what adding more sentences would do to
your week. It sits collapsed as a one-line summary until you tap it open,
waits until you have a few sentences in learning before it unlocks, and has
its own on/off switch in settings, separate from the due-count badges.
