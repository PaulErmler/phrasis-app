Flagging a card now asks what is wrong (translation, speaker gender, politeness, or something else) and lets you pick the right gender or politeness level for that card. Curriculum sentences are re-checked for who is speaking, so a sentence like "We are brothers" is no longer voiced or translated as if a woman said it. Every accepted flag earns 5 credits (up to 20 flags a month), with a small celebration to say thanks.
