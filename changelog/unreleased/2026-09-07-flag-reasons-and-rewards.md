Flagging a card now asks what is wrong (the translation, the speaker, or something else) and lets you say who should be speaking. Reporting the wrong speaker fixes the sentence for everyone: it is re-checked and re-translated for that voice, so "We are brothers" is no longer voiced or written as if a woman said it. Every accepted flag earns 5 credits (up to 20 flags a month), with a small celebration to say thanks.
