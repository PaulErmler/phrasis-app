The progress chart now starts at your running total and climbs to today's number, so the week and month views show your overall words, reviews and sentences instead of counting up from zero. Its year view shows one point per month over the last twelve months, and once you have two years of history it shows your whole journey in quarters. Words written with different apostrophes (J'aime, J’aime, J´aime) now count as one word in your vocabulary instead of two or three.
