Learning on a keyboard got faster: press R to restart the audio, T to replay the target-language audio, Shift+R to restart the whole sentence, and the left arrow to step back (it first takes back your last submitted answer, then returns to the previous sentence). Hover any control button to see its key, and find the full list behind the ? icon.
