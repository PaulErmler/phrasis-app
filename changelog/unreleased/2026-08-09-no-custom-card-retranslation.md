Cards you created yourself — through the chat, the add-cards form, or an import — could have their translations quietly rewritten. A card whose speaker gender was resolved after it was created would have its translation regenerated from the stored source sentence, sometimes replacing the exact vocabulary you'd added the card to learn. Your own cards are now never re-translated for any reason; only their audio is refreshed when the voice needs to change.
