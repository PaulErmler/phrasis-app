You can now choose whether your sentences speak as a male or female speaker — pick "Speaker voice & grammar" in the learning settings. In languages where the speaker's gender changes the wording (Spanish, Russian, Hebrew, Thai and many more), sentences are re-worded to match and every voice you hear follows your choice; "Mixed" keeps the familiar variety of voices. Sentences you added yourself keep their own speaker.
