When you correct the translation on a sentence that came from a course level, your version is no longer only saved to your own card. It is also passed to our translation model as a suggested improvement, so a genuinely better wording can reach everyone studying that sentence. Corrections you make in chat, and changes to the original sentence a card is translated from, are left out of this.
