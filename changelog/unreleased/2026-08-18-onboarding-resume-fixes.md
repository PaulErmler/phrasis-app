If you were partway through the old sign-up flow and had already finished (or
skipped) your first lesson, you now go straight to your dashboard instead of
being walked back through setup. Picking Shadowing at the end of setup also
correctly clears any writing style you had selected earlier, so switching to
Writing mode later starts you in Translate rather than Transcribe.
