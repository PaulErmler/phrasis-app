The chat tutor now offers one-tap quick actions under its answers — detailed grammar, verb conjugation, past/future forms, paraphrases, formal and simpler versions of the sentence you're learning. Tapping a word now also offers synonyms and antonyms, and after a writing exercise a new "Also correct?" button lets you ask the tutor whether your own wording was acceptable too.
