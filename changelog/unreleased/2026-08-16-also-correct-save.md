When you ask the tutor whether your own phrasing of a sentence is also correct — with the "Also correct?" button or just by asking in the chat — and it is, you can now save it right there: add it as a new card, or replace the card's text with your version (the audio updates to match, including the voice when the new phrasing calls for it).
