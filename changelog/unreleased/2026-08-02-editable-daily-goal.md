Your daily study goal is now front and center: a progress ring on the home screen fills up as you study and celebrates when you hit your goal for the day. Tap it to change your goal anytime — it's no longer fixed to what you picked during onboarding — or adjust it in the learning settings. New courses now ask for a daily goal as the last step of creation, and courses without one show a "Set daily goal" button in the ring's place.
