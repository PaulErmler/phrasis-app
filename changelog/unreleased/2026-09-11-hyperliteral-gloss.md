Sentences can now show a word-for-word gloss underneath: an English rendering that keeps the original word order, so you can see exactly what each word is doing ("Мне нравится гулять по городу вечером" becomes "To-me pleases to-walk around city in-evening"). It is on for new accounts, and everyone else can switch it on in learning settings.
