You can now choose English (UK), English (US) or English (Australia) as a language, next to the mixed-accent English. Mixed English really alternates British, American and Australian voices now, and each sentence keeps its accent (Spanish (Mixed) gets the same fix for Spain vs Latin America). English (UK) and English (Australia) courses show sentences in British or Australian wording (colour, lift, footpath), new cards on Mixed English read the wording that matches the accent they are spoken in, and that rewording changes only what marks the accent: meaning, tense and your own phrasing stay as they are.
