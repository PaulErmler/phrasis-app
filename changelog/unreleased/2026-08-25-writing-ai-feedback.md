Writing mode now gives you AI feedback on your answers: after you submit, a short verdict explains what was right and what slipped, and when your different wording is also correct it is saved as an accepted answer for that card — with its own audio, reading aids, and pronunciation shown alongside the card's sentence. Corrections are marked against whichever accepted answer you were closest to. You can also dictate your answer with the new microphone button next to the check mark, and the chat's "also correct" suggestions now save as accepted answers instead of rewriting the card. AI feedback can be turned off in the learning settings.
