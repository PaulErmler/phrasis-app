Writing mode now gives you AI feedback on your answers: after you submit, a short verdict explains what was right and what slipped, with corrections marked against whichever accepted answer you were closest to. When your different wording is also correct, it is saved as an accepted answer for that card — with its own audio, reading aids, and pronunciation — and AI feedback can be turned off in the learning settings.
