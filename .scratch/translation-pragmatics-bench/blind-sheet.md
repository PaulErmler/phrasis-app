# Blind pragmatics scoring sheet

Score every lettered candidate 0-10 for: (1) meaning and speech act kept,
(2) as general as the English (no added situation), (3) natural in the
named variety, (4) register/gender context respected.

---

## de-sa-everything-okay (speechAct)

**Source (en):** Everything okay?
**Intent:** A check-in question: the speaker asks whether the listener is alright or whether things are fine. Must stay a question; must not become a statement that everything is fine.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Alles in Ordnung?
- **B:** Alles okay?

---

## de-sa-you-good (speechAct)

**Source (en):** You good?
**Intent:** A casual check-in question ("are you okay?", "do you need anything?"). Must stay a question and stay casual; must not become a compliment or a statement.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Alles gut bei dir?
- **B:** Alles gut?

---

## de-sa-everything-all-right (speechAct)

**Source (en):** Everything all right with you?
**Intent:** A question about the listener’s wellbeing or situation in general. Must stay a question and must not pick one specific worry.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Ist bei dir alles in Ordnung?
- **B:** Alles in Ordnung bei dir?
- **C:** Ist alles in Ordnung bei dir?

---

## de-sa-coming-right (speechAct)

**Source (en):** You're coming, right?
**Intent:** A tag question seeking confirmation that the listener will come (to whatever was planned). Must stay a confirmation-seeking question; the event must not be invented.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Du kommst doch, oder?

---

## de-sa-are-you-sure (speechAct)

**Source (en):** Are you sure?
**Intent:** Asks the listener to confirm they are certain about what they just said or decided. Not about safety.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Bist du sicher?

---

## de-sa-how-come (speechAct)

**Source (en):** How come?
**Intent:** Asks for the reason ("why is that?"). Not about how someone arrived.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Wieso?
- **B:** Wie kommt’s?

---

## de-gen-thats-it (generality)

**Source (en):** That's it.
**Intent:** Signals completion or "that is all there is" (and can also mean "exactly"). Must stay as open as the English; must not become "that is the thing/object".
**Context:** speaker=neutral

- **A:** Das war's.
- **B:** Das ist alles.

---

## de-gen-it-happens (generality)

**Source (en):** It happens.
**Intent:** Consoling: such things happen, no big deal. Must not become "it is happening (right now)".
**Context:** speaker=neutral

- **A:** Das passiert.
- **B:** Das kommt vor.

---

## de-gen-same-here (generality)

**Source (en):** Same here.
**Intent:** The speaker shares the same state, feeling or opinion ("me too"). Must not invent what is being shared.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Geht mir genauso.

---

## de-gen-let-me-know (generality)

**Source (en):** Let me know.
**Intent:** Asks the listener to inform the speaker later (about whatever is at issue). No specific topic may be added.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Sag mir Bescheid.
- **B:** Gib mir Bescheid.

---

## de-gen-on-my-way (generality)

**Source (en):** I'm on my way.
**Intent:** The speaker has set off and is coming. Must not become a literal statement about being on a road, and no destination may be added.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Ich bin unterwegs.

---

## de-gen-sounds-good (generality)

**Source (en):** Sounds good.
**Intent:** Agreement with, or acceptance of, a proposal ("fine by me"). Not about acoustics or music.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Klingt gut.

---

## de-idiom-take-care (idiomFunction)

**Source (en):** Take care.
**Intent:** A parting wish, a farewell. Not an instruction to be careful about something in particular.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Pass auf dich auf.
- **B:** Mach's gut.

---

## de-idiom-never-mind (idiomFunction)

**Source (en):** Never mind.
**Intent:** Retracts or dismisses what was just said ("forget it", "it doesn’t matter"). Not "do not think" and not "do not worry about your problem".
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Schon gut.

---

## de-idiom-ill-pass (idiomFunction)

**Source (en):** I'll pass.
**Intent:** Politely declines an offer or invitation ("no thanks"). Not passing by, passing an exam, or passing something along.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Ich passe.

---

## de-idiom-you-tell-me (idiomFunction)

**Source (en):** You tell me.
**Intent:** Turns the question back on the listener: "you would know better than I would" / "I have no idea". Not a request to be told something specific.
**Context:** speaker=neutral, addressee=unspecified, register=neutral

- **A:** Sag du es mir.

---

## de-reg-send-file-formal (register)

**Source (en):** Could you send me the file by tomorrow?
**Intent:** A polite request to a woman the speaker is formal with. Must use the polite/formal address form and stay a request, not an order.
**Context:** speaker=male, addressee=female, register=formal

- **A:** Könnten Sie mir die Datei bis morgen schicken?

---

## de-reg-sit-down-informal (register)

**Source (en):** Sit down, we need to talk.
**Intent:** A casual imperative to a man the speaker is close to, followed by a serious-sounding announcement. Must use the casual address form.
**Context:** speaker=female, addressee=male, register=informal

- **A:** Setz dich, wir müssen reden.
